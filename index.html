A little about us

Growing curious minds, one day at a time.

School is about more than lessons. It is also where children begin to find their voices, form friendships and discover what they can do.

At Oakpark Academy, learning brings together classroom experiences, personal development and activities that give children room to discover their interests. We aim to create an environment where learners can develop academically and socially as they progress through school.

Our school serves families looking for kindergarten, primary and junior school education in the Uthiru area of Nairobi.

Learning at Oakpark

Every stage has its own journey.

Children change as they grow. Our learning stages provide a clear way for families to explore the school experience that suits their child's age and next step.

01 · EARLY YEARS

Kindergarten

The early years are a time for asking questions, discovering new things, making friends and building a foundation for future learning.

02 · BUILDING FOUNDATIONS

Primary School

A stage for strengthening core skills, developing independent thinking and building the confidence to take on new challenges.

03 · THE NEXT STEP

Junior School

As learners grow, they have more opportunities to develop responsibility, broaden their knowledge and prepare for the next phase of education.

Character

Helping learners develop respect, responsibility and consideration for the people around them.

Curiosity

Encouraging children to ask questions, try new things and engage actively with what they learn.

Community

Recognising the important role that children, families and educators play in a child's school experience.

Beyond the classroom

There is more to school than a desk and a book.

Some of the most memorable learning experiences happen when children move, create, practise a skill or try something unfamiliar. Co-curricular activities can give learners a chance to discover interests beyond their regular lessons.

Oakpark's school activities include opportunities such as sports, music and other enrichment experiences. Families can contact the school to learn which activities are currently available.
